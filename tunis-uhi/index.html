Tunis Heat Twin

The surface temperature of Greater Tunis seen by Landsat 8, explained by land cover, then used to test what planting trees would do. Each step follows the DTT4SD hands-on notebook by Bruno Ćaleta (Oikon).

Data. Landsat 8 Collection 1 Level-1, band 10, from the Google Cloud public archive (USGS). ESA WorldCover 2021 v200, 10 m (© ESA WorldCover project, CC BY 4.0). Grid: 90 m, UTM zone 32N, 10.00–10.35° E, 36.65–36.95° N.

How this differs from the notebook. The notebook runs on Google Earth Engine with Landsat Level-2 temperature, Dynamic World probabilities, summer 2025 and a live feed. This page is built without an Earth Engine account. It uses the open 2021 Landsat archive, computes temperature from the thermal band with an NDVI emissivity correction and no atmospheric correction, and uses the share of each WorldCover class inside a 90 m cell where the notebook uses Dynamic World probabilities. Land cover is one 2021 map, so the shadow step cannot refresh it.

Method and teaching material: brunocalet/DTT4SD-Tunis-UHI, DTT4SD workshop, Tunis, 10 November 2026 (text and figures CC BY 4.0, code MIT). The temperatures are of surfaces at about 10:55 local time, not of the air.
