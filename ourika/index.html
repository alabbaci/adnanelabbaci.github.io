Ourika Watch — how it works

A live dashboard for the Ourika watershed upstream of the Aghbalou gauge: a steep High Atlas basin south of Marrakech, known for violent summer flash floods.

What is measured, what is modelled

  • Basin, sub-basins, rivers, profile — derived from the Copernicus GLO-30 elevation model (D8 flow routing). The delineated area is km².
  • Rain, snow, temperature, wind — Open-Meteo forecast, sampled at each sub-basin’s mean elevation. Model output, not gauge readings.
  • River discharge — GloFAS global flood model via Open-Meteo, on a 5 km cell. It follows trends; absolute values on a basin this small are rough.
  • Earthquakes — USGS catalogue, live.
  • Satellite — NASA GIBS daily MODIS imagery.
  • Villages, roads, buildings — Overture Maps. “Exposed” buildings lie within m of a channel draining at least km².
