      About Mini Rabat 3D

      A 3D digital map of public transport in Rabat–Salé, in the spirit of Mini Tokyo 3D. Trams and trains move along their real tracks according to a timetable, in Morocco local time.

      What's real, what's simulated

      • Real: track geometry, stop order and station positions for tram lines L1 and L2 and the ONCF main line, from OpenStreetMap.
      • Simulated: departure times. No public real-time feed exists for Rabat, so vehicles follow a timetable built from typical headways (tram roughly every 8–15 min, 06:00–22:00) and typical ONCF service patterns (TNR shuttles, Al Atlas intercity, Al Boraq). Positions show how the network runs, not where a specific vehicle is right now.
      • Vehicles are drawn larger than life when zoomed out so they stay visible.

      Tips

      • Click a tram or train to follow it. Esc stops following.
      • Click a station for its next departures.
      • Drag to pan; right-drag or Ctrl-drag to rotate and tilt.
