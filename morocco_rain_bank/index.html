Morocco's rain bank, 2002/03 – 2025/26

Rain above or below the 1991–2020 normal, added up season by season since 2002/03. Height and colour show the same value: blue rises where Morocco has banked a surplus, red sinks where the deficit has built up. ERA5 precipitation; not a groundwater measurement.
