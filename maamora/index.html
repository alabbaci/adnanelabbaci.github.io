      Method and limits

      This page follows the pattern of Microsoft's AI Workflow for Planetary Computer Pro: fix an area and a time range, pull the matching imagery from a catalogue, split the area into processing units, run the analysis, and catalogue the outputs as STAC items. Its change layer borrows the output shape of the MARS model: vector polygons, each with a category and a confidence.

      MARS itself is not used here. It extracts buildings, roads and railways and does not measure vegetation, and AI Workflow is a private preview that needs an Azure subscription with GPU quota. The analysis on this page is the NDVI index and plain statistics, run on open data.

      How the numbers are made

      • NDVI = (near infrared − red) / (near infrared + red) from Sentinel-2 bands 8 and 4. Each month uses up to three of the clearest scenes; clouds, shadows and snow are removed with the scene classification band, and the highest NDVI per pixel is kept.
      • Anomaly compares a month with the mean of the same calendar month over 2019–2023. Five years is a short reference and it includes dry years.
      • Trend and Change use July–September only. In the dry season the grass layer is brown, so NDVI then mostly reflects tree canopy.
      • Change polygons are patches of at least 3 ha where summer NDVI moved by 0.12 or more between the first three summers and the latest one. Confidence compares that movement with the spread of change across the whole forest, and is cut by up to 40% where the previous summer does not show the change too.

      What it cannot tell you

      • NDVI measures greenness, not tree health or species. A loss polygon can be a eucalyptus harvest, a fire, dieback, clearing or drought stress; the page does not know which.
      • The forest outline comes from OpenStreetMap polygons and is not the official forestry boundary. It includes eucalyptus, pine and acacia plantations as well as cork oak.
      • Pixels are about 70 m. Single trees and narrow tracks are not resolved.
      • Nothing here has been checked on the ground.
