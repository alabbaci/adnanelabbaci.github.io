Morocco, fifty years of rainfall

Every rainy season from 1976/77 to 2025/26 (September to August), compared with the 1991–2020 normal and draped over Morocco's relief with forge3d. The map shows the Standardized Precipitation Index; the numbers give each season's rainfall in mm and as a share of normal.

Data: ECMWF ERA5 (Copernicus Climate Change Service) via WeatherBench 2 and ARCO-ERA5 · Elevation: Tilezen Terrarium · Rendered with forge3d · Method and code
