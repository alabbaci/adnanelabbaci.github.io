    How these numbers are made

    Dakhla has no openly published neighbourhood boundaries or neighbourhood statistics. Everything here is computed from open data, and each figure says how.

    Zones

    The zones are analysis units, not administrative districts. Building footprints are clustered, each cluster takes the land nearest its centre, and the result is clipped to the built-up area. A zone is named after its longest named street, or its position relative to the centre of the built city.

    Indicators

      Limits

      • Population by zone is modelled: the 2024 census total for the city is shared out by residential building volume. It is not a census count per zone.
      • The building layer is coarse in places: several footprints cover a whole block. Counts of buildings are therefore low; areas and volumes are more reliable.
      • Vegetation is what a 10 m satellite pixel can see in spring. Small gardens and single trees fall below it.
      • Only vegetation has a year-by-year record. The other indicators are single snapshots, and the timeline says so.
