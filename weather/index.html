A FIELD GUIDE

Explore the weather.

Choose a highlight, then switch between its forecast scenes. Press play or drag the timeline to follow the pattern. Move over the map to read a value. All times are UTC.

What am I seeing?

Maps use ECMWF forecasts. Rain and gust scenes summarize the hours ending at the displayed time. Ensemble charts show the percentage of model members exceeding the stated threshold; a probability is not a guarantee. Warmth anomalies refer to 850 hPa air, roughly 1.5 km up.

How highlights are chosen

A Python sweep detects and ranks heat, rain, wind, storm potential, waves and other notable patterns across Africa and nearby seas. It selects up to 15 items, including a continent-wide explainer. The included automation checks for new data twice daily.

Reading the map

Colors show the variable in the legend. White arrows show forecast surface wind where available. Light lines show pressure at 4 hPa intervals. Missing data remain uncolored.

These are model forecasts, not observed conditions or official warnings. For safety decisions, consult your national weather service.
